Step 1 — see what your checkpoints actually are

.venv\Scripts\python.exe -c "import torch,glob,os; [print(os.path.basename(p), {k:v for k,v in torch.load(p,map_location='cpu',weights_only=False).items() if k not in ('model_state_dict','optimizer_state_dict','scheduler_state_dict')}) for p in glob.glob('upgrade/checkpoints/*.pth')]"
This prints each checkpoint's epoch, val_mae_m, and val_maes_by_landscape. If val_maes_by_landscape mentions open_canopy, that one is the OC blend; if it's {gamus: ...} only, it's still pure GAMUS.

Step 2 — confirm its quality on the honest test set

.venv\Scripts\python.exe upgrade\evaluation\eval_test.py --ckpt upgrade\checkpoints\metric_best.pth --limit 200
If aggregate ≈ 2.11 m / tall ≈ 3.80 m, it's GAMUS-grade — use it as the base with a clear conscience. (It won't be materially worse; the OC epochs barely moved it.)

Step 3 — back it up NOW (so you don't lose it again)

copy upgrade\checkpoints\metric_best.pth upgrade\checkpoints\metric_gamus_best.pth

Step 4 — run the combined blend from that base, into a fresh dir

.venv\Scripts\python.exe upgrade\training\train_metric.py --offline --blend `
  --oc-root D:\Depth-Wizard\Open-Canopy --dfc-root D:\Depth-Wizard\DFC2023 `
  --gamus-root D:\Depth-Wizard\GAMUS `
  --init-weights upgrade\checkpoints\metric_gamus_best.pth `
  --ckpt-dir upgrade\checkpoints\blend_oc_dfc `
  --epochs 15 --warmup 2 --enc-lr 2.5e-6 --head-lr 2.5e-5 `
  --batch 2 --grad-accum 8 --crop 504 --aux-fraction 0.3 `
  --aux-weights "open_canopy=1.0,dfc2023=1.0" --test-tiles 40 --workers 0

The --ckpt-dir blend_oc_dfc keeps this run's checkpoints separate so your base is never clobbered again.
